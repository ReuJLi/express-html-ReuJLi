Mission Statement

Seasider Sports & Activities engages students in fun student-led activities & programs that enable students to succeed: physically, socially, mentally, and spiritually.

Seasider Sports & Activities provides a wide variety of fun, safe, and wholesome sporting events and activities for all BYU–Hawaii Ohana. These events include open gym/free play, single-day tournaments, and intramural leagues. As well as dances, movies, traditions, concerts, and opening and closing socials. Come get connected through all levels of play and fun activities every week. Don’t miss out on the action!

Policies

  • Honor Code
    All students are required to conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the Honor Code. In addition, students may not influence or seek to influence others to engage in behavior inconsistent with the Honor Code.
  • Dress and Grooming Standards
    The attire and grooming of patrons must always be modest, neat, clean, and aligned to the principles and expectations of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints and Brigham Young University–Hawaii. All students are required to follow the Dress and Grooming Standards in order to participate in any sports or recreational activity.
