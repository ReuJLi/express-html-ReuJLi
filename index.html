Mission Statement

Seasider Sports & Activities engages students in fun student-led activities & programs that enable students to succeed: physically, socially, mentally, and spiritually.

Seasider Sports & Activities provides a wide variety of fun, safe, and wholesome sporting events and activities for all BYU–Hawaii Ohana. These events include open gym/free play, single-day tournaments, and intramural leagues. As well as dances, movies, traditions, concerts, and opening and closing socials. Come get connected through all levels of play and fun activities every week. Don’t miss out on the action!
